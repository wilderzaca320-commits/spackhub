## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a speculative, hyper-modern digital art laboratory and exhibition space. It merges the serene, diffuse atmosphere of ambient light art with the surgical precision of speculative design documentation and high-fashion digital editorial design.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges:
- **Atmospheric Iridescence:** A living backdrop shifting imperceptibly between ethereal lavender, cryogenic ice blue, and faint mineral peach/rose mist.
- **Micro-Technical Precision:** Utilitarian, monospaced indices, technical coordinates, catalog markers (`FD.EXP`, `KŌBŌ`, `ORL 6QX`, `@2026`), and razor-thin hairlines balanced against wide typographic pauses.
- **Floating Tactility:** Rich, saturated multimedia viewports suspended as rounded, frosted acrylic tiles over soft fog, avoiding heavy dropped shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and translucent depth.
- **Audience:** Digital artists, creative technologists, curators, avant-garde design studios, and web3 visual researchers who prize restrained, high-concept UI.

## Colors

The palette relies on a luminous, multidirectional canvas gradient punctuated by near-black carbon text, surgical hair-lines, and electric focal accents.

- **Canvas Gradient Mesh:**
  - Top-Left / Sky: Lavender Mist `#C3B8F8` fading into Ice Cyan `#C7E8FA`
  - Center / Horizon: Silver Frost `#DFDBEC` blending with Muted Orchid `#E2CFF4`
  - Bottom / Ground: Warm Dawn Peach `#FCD2C9` shifting toward Pale Seafoam `#BCE6DE`
- **Primary Ink (`#111317`):** Pure carbon black for high-contrast alphanumeric titles, typographic monuments, and structural grid rules.
- **Secondary Accent (`#6B46FF`):** Hyper-saturated ultraviolet used for active states, interactive tags, and focal telemetry markers.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#FF4D6D`):** Neon vermilion/coral used sparingly for experimental warnings, live signals, and media highlights.
- **Neutral Border & Subtext (`#111317` at 20%–45% opacity / `#555268`):** Semi-transparent carbon and slate values that provide low-interference structure without breaking the canvas glow.
- **Frosted Translucency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`):** Container fills that preserve underlying hue motion.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is dual-axis:
1. **Primary Structural Text (`Space Grotesk`):** Open, geometric proportions with distinct modernist cutouts. Employed in wide, uppercase, or spaced regular weights for project identities (`ORL 6QX`), gallery titles, and clean descriptions.
2. **Technical Telemetry (`Space Mono`):** Monospaced, zero-compromise micro-copy. Rendered exclusively in uppercase for indexes, metadata, mode switches, audio triggers (`SFX`), and chronological footers (`@2026`). Letter spacing is deliberately expanded (`0.12em` to `0.22em`) to mirror archival blueprints and architectural schematics.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas functions as a 3D-feel exhibition stage framed by tactical peripheral coordinates:

- **Perimeter Anchor Band:** Crucial utility nodes are pinned along the absolute outer edges—`FD.EXP` and logo glyph at top center, navigational coordinates (`LIST/GRID`, `ABOUT`, `LET'S TALK`) at top flanks, and system metadata (`SFX`, `EXPRESSION HUB`, `KŌBŌ`, `@2026`) aligned across the bottom rail.
- **Stage Center Carousel / Gallery Strip:** A horizontal stage containing high-density rounded cards. Desktop uses an overflowing horizontal flex row with subtle perspective curvature or flat parallax scrolling; mobile reflows to an edge-to-edge kinetic horizontal peek slider.
- **Grid Structure:** When in grid mode, adopts a 12-column layout with 32px gutters and 40px outer safe margins.
- **Rhythm:** Dense clusters of micro-metadata (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) surrounded by vast negative space (`space-xl` and above) to create an airy, unhurried gallery cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on chromatic glass tiers, edge refraction, and glowing optical layers:

- **Surface 0 (Atmosphere):** Continuous, animated linear-gradient mesh that subtly shifts hue coordinates.
- **Surface 1 (Floating Acrylic Cards):** 
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(140%)`
  - Outline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)` with an inner specular glow of `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`
  - Shadow: `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(107, 70, 255, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`
- **Surface 2 (System HUD Overlays):** Interactive micro-menus and inspection loupes utilize sharp hairline containers (`1px solid rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.15)`) floating freely over content with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Active State Flare:** Hovered or focused exhibits trigger an ambient edge luminescence in neon violet (`#6B46FF`) or coral (`#FF4D6D`) using `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(107, 70, 255, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The geometry pairs generous curvature for visual media with razor-sharp linear discipline for interactive metadata:

- **Exhibition Tiles & Media Windows:** Defined with `roundedness: 2` (16px / `1rem` base border radius; large hero viewports scale to 24px–28px). These forms mimic polished obsidian and cut rounded glass prisms.
- **Control Pills & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) or strictly squared micro-tags (`2px` border radius) to maintain a mechanical contrast against rounded media portals.
- **Hairline Dividers:** 1px stroke rules with variable horizontal opacity (`0%` -> `30%` -> `0%`) acting as architectural guides.

## Components

### Micro-Nav & System Badges
- **Structure:** Text-first minimal controls set in `Space Mono` uppercase.
- **Interaction:** Unadorned or framed with a `1px` translucent pill border. Hover induces an arrow glyph shift (e.g., `↓ KŌBŌ`, `→ BLACK`) or an underline stroke extending from 0 to 100% width.

### Exhibition Viewport Cards
- **Visuals:** High-density rounded rectangles hosting video, 3D renders, or graphic experiments.
- **Micro-Headers:** Floated directly above each card corner in 10px monospace uppercase (`MTN CPTR`, `3D FLLS`, `GRL FLX`), accompanied by subtle live status dots or coordinate strings.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Tall portrait (`3:4`) or squircle (`1:1.15`) profiles, floating synchronously along the horizontal viewport plane.

### Scrubber & Precision Slider
- **Bar:** Ultra-fine 1px carbon track (`rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.25)`).
- **Thumb:** Minimal vertical tick (`1px × 8px` dark carbon bar) or miniature polygon icon indicating current catalog progression.

### Audio & Mode Toggles (`SFX`, `LIST/GRID`)
- **Visuals:** Minimalist glyph or segmented text toggle separated by a slash (`/`).
- **Active Indicator:** Color shift to Primary Ink (`#111317`) with a miniature saturated neon accent beacon (2px dot).

### Inputs & Search Terminals
- **Visuals:** Underline-only or borderless translucent input pill with `Space Mono` placeholder text (`INPUT QUERY //`).
- **Focus:** Border transitions to `rgba(107, 70, 255, 0.8)` accompanied by an ethereal backdrop pulse.

### Monolithic Title Display (`ORL 6QX`)
- **Structure:** Centered large-scale typography paired with floating geometric sub-glyphs (e.g., spinning multifaceted prisms or asterisks) positioned between word breaks.
